# Local Model Calculator Privacy Policy

**Publisher:** SMMOULDER PRODUCTIONS  
**Effective date:** October 20, 2026

This policy covers Local Model Calculator, also called Local AI Workbench in its interface, including its Windows desktop app and browser-based calculator.

## Information processed on your device

Memory estimates and calculator controls are processed on your device. The app saves calculator settings, selected model metadata, theme preference, and setup-panel state in local application or browser storage so they can be restored later.

If you choose a Windows DxDiag text report or a macOS System Information XML report, the app reads and parses it locally to identify relevant hardware information. Reports can contain device names and other identifying information. The app does not upload or save the report itself. Hardware values used to populate calculator settings can be saved with those settings.

The app does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, or developer tracking. It does not download or run AI model weights and does not generate AI content.

## Connections to Hugging Face

When the app opens, it automatically requests public model metadata from Hugging Face to populate suggested starting points. Searching, filtering, inspecting, or selecting models can make additional requests for public repository listings, model file sizes, and model configuration metadata.

These HTTPS requests send the relevant search or filter choices and repository identifiers to Hugging Face. As with other internet connections, Hugging Face receives network information such as your IP address and ordinary request headers. The app's model-metadata requests omit authentication credentials and do not require a Hugging Face login or access token.

Calculator settings and imported hardware reports are not uploaded to Hugging Face. Manual memory calculations remain available without an internet connection; online suggestions and discovery require connectivity.

Hugging Face handles information under its [privacy policy](https://huggingface.co/privacy).

## External links and optional donations

Documentation, model cards, project support, and donation links can open in your default browser. Those websites operate independently and apply their own privacy policies. Visiting a model download page is separate from the calculator's metadata requests.

The optional “Buy me a coffee” button opens [buymeacoffee.com/smmoulder](https://buymeacoffee.com/smmoulder). Donations do not unlock app features. The calculator does not collect or receive payment-card details. Payment and account information you provide to that service is governed by [Buy Me a Coffee's privacy policy](https://buymeacoffee.com/privacy-policy).

## Storage, retention, and your choices

Local preferences remain on your device until changed or cleared. The embedded web runtime or your browser may also cache public metadata and network responses. We do not operate a server that receives your calculator settings, hardware reports, or discovery requests.

The calculator's Reset button resets calculation choices; it does not clear every appearance preference or runtime cache. You can clear browser site data for the browser version, or use Windows application storage/reset controls where available for the packaged desktop app. Uninstalling a portable app or deleting its executable may leave application data on the device.

We do not sell app data or use it for advertising. HTTPS protects metadata requests in transit, and local storage relies on your operating system's protections. No storage or transmission method is completely secure.

## Microsoft Store and Windows

Microsoft may process installation, purchase, and diagnostic information through the Microsoft Store or Windows under the [Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement). This is separate from the calculator's local processing.

## Children

The app does not request names, email addresses, accounts, or other personal information from children. It is not designed to collect personal information from children.

## Contact and support

For privacy questions and support, contact SMMOULDER PRODUCTIONS through the [Local Calculator GitHub issue tracker](https://github.com/smmoulder/Local_Calculator/issues).

GitHub issues are public. Do not post hardware reports, credentials, contact details, or other confidential information. If you submit an issue, GitHub and the publisher can see the information you choose to provide. GitHub's handling of that information is described in the [GitHub General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Policy changes

We will publish policy changes on this page and update the effective date. Material changes to the app's data practices will be reflected in this policy.
