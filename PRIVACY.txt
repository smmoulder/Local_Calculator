Local AI Workbench privacy notice — draft for publisher review

Calculations and preferences are processed on your computer. Preferences
are saved in the app's local storage. Hardware reports you choose are
parsed locally; their contents are not uploaded or saved by the app.
The app does not include analytics, advertising, or developer tracking.

Using model discovery sends your search text, selected repository names,
and metadata requests directly to Hugging Face over HTTPS. Hugging Face
receives network information such as your IP address and applies its own
privacy policy: https://huggingface.co/privacy. No login or access token
is required. The app does not download model weights.

External documentation and optional donation links open in your browser.
Those services have their own privacy policies. The app never receives
payment-card details. Local settings remain on the computer until cleared
through the operating system or app storage. Uninstall may retain local
application data depending on how the app was installed.

Support and privacy inquiries:
https://github.com/smmoulder/Local_Calculator/issues
Do not post personal hardware reports or private data to public issues.

Publisher: review this notice and publish it at a stable HTTPS URL before
Store submission. Replace this draft footer with an effective date.
